## Description

PACT helps small teams check the promises they make before their schedules break.

Describe a business in plain language, review an editable AI setup and its
assumptions, then check whether the team's work fits its available hours. In this
demo, fictional burger shop BunStop needs six hours of work but has only four
eligible hours on Day 1. PACT proposes moving flexible training to Day 2; a person
approves the change and later recovers the decision with supporting sources.

Built by Rujul Talekar, solo, for Graph Hacks / FalkorDB. Tracks: Agents That Act

This video combines genuine deployed interactions with labelled explanatory
graphics. The captured setup uses one real AI request. The subsequent planning
check and memory recall use deterministic rules; separate dated evidence covers
earlier live agent coordination. These counts describe this capture, not total
project usage. Narration is locally generated Kokoro neural speech. AI development
assistance is disclosed in the repository.

All business data is fictional. PACT is a bounded two-day staff-and-task planning
prototype. Approval records internal reservations, not external execution. It is
not a POS, inventory or live-order system.
